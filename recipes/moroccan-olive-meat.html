Moroccan Olive Meat Dish

This is the kind of dish that fills a kitchen with the smell of something serious happening on the stove. Briny green olives, mellowed by a soak in hot water, slow-cooked with fatty meat in a deeply spiced tomato sauce built on turmeric, paprika, and garlic. It rewards patience — the longer it cooks, the better it gets.

Prep 15 min
Cook 1–3 hrs
Servings 4–6
Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 can green olives, drained
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 4–5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tbsp paprika
  • ½ cup water (plus more as needed)
  • 1.5–2 lbs fatty meat (lamb shoulder, beef cheek, or short rib)
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Tip: The fattier the cut, the better. Lean meat will dry out during the long cook. Lamb shoulder or beef short rib are ideal.

Instructions

  1. 01

    De-salt the olives

    Drain the can of green olives and place them in a bowl of hot water. Let them soak for 10–15 minutes to draw out the excess saltiness from the brine. Drain and set aside. This step makes a real difference — canned olives straight from the can can overpower the dish.

  2. 02

    Prepare the meat

    You have two options here. For the fastest result, cook the meat in a pressure cooker with a pinch of salt until tender — about 40 minutes — then add it to the sauce in the final stage. Alternatively, add the raw meat directly to the sauce in step 4 and let everything cook together low and slow for 2–3 hours until the meat falls apart. Both work; the pressure cooker method is more convenient, the slow method builds more depth.

  3. 03

    Build the sauce

    Heat oil in a wide, heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat. Add the tomato paste and let it fry in the oil for 1–2 minutes, stirring constantly, until it darkens slightly and smells fragrant. This step — cooking the paste rather than just adding it — removes the raw, acidic edge and adds a richer base flavor. Add the garlic and stir for another 30 seconds.

  4. 04

    Spice and simmer

    Add the sugar, turmeric, and paprika to the pot and stir to combine with the tomato paste. Pour in the water and stir until you have a smooth, fragrant sauce. Add the drained olives and stir to coat. If adding raw meat, nestle the pieces into the sauce now. Season with salt and pepper, cover, and cook on low heat — 2–3 hours if the meat is raw, or 20–30 minutes if it was pre-cooked in the pressure cooker.

  5. 05

    Finish and serve

    The dish is ready when the sauce has thickened and the meat is completely tender, pulling apart with a spoon. Taste and adjust salt. Serve over white rice or with crusty bread to scoop up the sauce.

Chef's note

The sugar isn't there to make it sweet — it balances the bitterness of the olives and the acidity of the tomato paste. Don't skip it, but don't add more than called for either.
